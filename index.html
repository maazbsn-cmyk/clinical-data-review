What is this tool?

The Clinical Decision Support System is an academic triage and evaluation tool designed to assist with:

  • AI Case Analysis: Extracting key findings and prioritizing evidence-based interventions from raw patient data or ward sheets.
  • Clinical Calculators: Real-time assessment of GCS, BMI, MAP, Maintenance Fluids, and IV Drip rates with instant guideline generation.

⚠️ Clinical Use Disclaimer

By proceeding, you strictly acknowledge and agree to the following:

  • This application is an experimental academic tool designed for educational purposes.
  • It does not replace professional medical judgment, clinical diagnosis, or attending physician orders.
  • All AI-generated triage protocols and calculator outputs must be independently verified against established hospital protocols prior to clinical application.

🚨 Emergency Protocols

  • Chest Pain: ECG within 10 min, check O2, Aspirin 325mg, Sublingual Nitroglycerin.
  • Stroke (CVA): Fast-track non-contrast CT head within 20 min of arrival.
  • Anaphylaxis: Immediate IM Epinephrine (1:1000) 0.5mg into mid-outer thigh.
  • Seizure/Status: Secure airway, IV Lorazepam, check blood glucose.

🩸 MAP Target Ranges

  • Formula: (SBP + 2(DBP)) / 3
  • Normal Range: 70 – 100 mmHg
  • Sepsis Target: ≥ 65 mmHg
  • Critical Perfusion: < 65 mmHg (Indicates high risk of ischemia, shock, and organ hypoperfusion).

💧 4-2-1 Rule Breakdown

  • First 10 kg: 4 mL/kg/hr
  • Next 10 kg (11-20kg): Add 2 mL/kg/hr
  • Each kg > 20 kg: Add 1 mL/kg/hr
  • Standard calculation for baseline hourly maintenance IV fluid rates in pediatric and adult patients.

⏱️ IV Drop Factors

  • Formula: (Total Volume in mL × Drop Factor) / Time in Minutes
  • Macrodrip Tubing: Typically delivers large drops (10, 15, or 20 gtt/mL). Used for rapid fluid delivery or thick fluids.
  • Microdrip Tubing: Always delivers 60 gtt/mL. Used for pediatric patients or precise, slow medication drips.
